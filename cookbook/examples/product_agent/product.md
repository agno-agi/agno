# Acme Reports

Acme Reports helps teams create and share reports from their project data.

## Export a report

Open a saved report, select Export, and choose CSV. The export includes
only rows that match the report's current filters.

## Schedule a report

Open a saved report and select Schedule. Choose a daily or weekly schedule,
set a delivery time, and add the recipients' email addresses.

Scheduled reports are available on the Team plan. Workspace admins
can create and edit schedules.
